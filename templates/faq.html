Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Solo Parent DSS and RA 11861.

About the System

The Solo Parent Decision Support System (DSS) is an AI-powered eligibility screening tool designed to help solo parent applicants and MSWDO staff assess eligibility under Republic Act 11861 — the Solo Parent Welfare Act. It provides a fast, consistent first-pass result before the official manual review.

No. This system is for demonstration and pre-screening purposes only. It does not replace the official MSWDO application process. Applicants must still visit their local MSWDO office to submit documents and complete formal registration. The result generated here is an advisory assessment, not an official determination.

This system may be used by:
  • Solo parent applicants — to check initial eligibility before going to the MSWDO office.
  • MSWDO/LGU staff — to triage and pre-screen applications faster.
  • Researchers and students — to study AI-assisted welfare eligibility systems.
Eligibility & Benefits

A solo parent is any individual who falls under at least one of these situations:
  • Spouse has died and has not remarried
  • Legally separated, annulled, or abandoned by spouse
  • Unmarried parent bearing/raising a child
  • Any person who solely provides parental care to a child
  • Parent whose spouse is incarcerated or incapacitated

Refer to Section 3 of RA 11861 for the full definition.

  • ₱1,000 Monthly Cash Subsidy — for those earning minimum wage or below (Sec. 15a)
  • VAT Exemption & Discounts — on goods for children aged 0–6 (Sec. 15b)
  • PhilHealth Coverage — priority enrollment
  • Livelihood & Housing — priority access to government programs
  • Educational Support — scholarships and tuition assistance (Sec. 9)
  • Flexible Work Arrangements — right to request from employers

Privacy & Data
