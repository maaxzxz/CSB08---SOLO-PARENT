Key Provisions Implemented in This System

Section 3 — Definition

The system evaluates all statutory solo parent situations including unmarried parents, widows/widowers, separated/annulled individuals, abandoned spouses, parents of children with disabilities, and those whose spouses are detained or incapacitated.

Section 7 — Solo Parent ID

Eligibility results from this system serve as a pre-screening guide for the official Solo Parent ID application, which must still be processed through the local MSWDO.

Section 9 — Educational Support

Scholarship and educational assistance eligibility is flagged in assessment results for qualified solo parents with children of school age.

Section 15a — Cash Subsidy

The ₱1,000 monthly cash subsidy check applies income-based criteria assessed in the form — available to those earning the minimum wage or below.

Section 15b — VAT & Discounts

Discount eligibility on child-care goods for children aged 0–6 is computed based on the child's age fields entered in the assessment form.

Section 18 — Parental Leave

Solo parents with at least one year of employment are entitled to 7 days of parental leave. Employment status inputs in the form are used to flag this benefit.
