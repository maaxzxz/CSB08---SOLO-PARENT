Republic Act 11861

The Solo Parent Welfare Act ensures comprehensive support to qualified solo parents.

  • ₱1,000 Monthly Cash Subsidy - For solo parents earning minimum wage or below (Sec. 15a)
  • VAT Exemption & Discounts - On select child-care goods for children aged 0-6 (Sec. 15b)
  • Priority Access - PhilHealth coverage, livelihood, and housing priority
  • Educational Support - Scholarship and tuition assistance programs (Sec. 9)
