Documents to Bring

Prepare the following before visiting your MSWDO office:

  • Duly accomplished application form
  • Birth certificate of child/children (PSA copy)
  • Valid government-issued ID
  • Proof of income (payslip, Certificate of Employment, or sworn affidavit)
  • Death certificate of spouse (if applicable)
  • Certificate of legal separation/annulment (if applicable)
  • Barangay certification of residency
  • 2x2 ID photos (2 copies)
