Privacy Policy

How this system handles your data — and what it does not do.

Last Updated: July 2025

Anonymous

No personal identity is collected or linked to results.

Encrypted

All connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.

Not Stored

Form data is discarded immediately after your session ends.

1. Purpose of This System

The Solo Parent Decision Support System (DSS) is a demonstration tool developed for academic and public service research purposes. It is designed to assist solo parent applicants and MSWDO staff in performing a preliminary eligibility screening based on the criteria set under Republic Act 11861 (Solo Parent Welfare Act of 2022).

2. Data Collection

This system does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) in a persistent manner. While form fields may ask for names, dates, and household details for the purpose of assessment, this data is:

  • Processed locally in server memory only during the active session
  • Not transmitted to any external database or third-party server
  • Automatically discarded once the session ends or the browser is closed
  • Never logged in any form to disk or persistent storage
3. Data Sharing

This system does not share, sell, or transmit any user input to third parties. No analytics services, advertising networks, or government databases are connected to this tool in its demonstration state.

4. Cookies & Tracking

This system uses only functional session cookies necessary for the form to operate (e.g., maintaining state across pages within a session). No tracking cookies, behavioral profiling, or cross-site analytics are used.

5. Legal Basis

This system is operated in accordance with:

  • Republic Act 10173 — Data Privacy Act of 2012
  • Republic Act 11861 — Expanded Solo Parent Welfare Act of 2022
  • National Privacy Commission (NPC) guidelines on privacy by design
6. Your Rights

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Be informed of how your data is used (this policy)
  • Access any data that may have been processed
  • Erasure — since no data is stored, there is nothing to erase
  • Object to any data processing — simply close the browser
7. Contact for Privacy Concerns

For privacy-related concerns about this demonstration system, you may raise an inquiry via the Report Inquiry page, or contact the National Privacy Commission (NPC) directly at www.privacy.gov.ph.

Disclaimer: This privacy policy applies only to this demonstration system. It does not represent the privacy practices of the DSWD, any LGU, or any official government information system. Always consult with the appropriate office before submitting sensitive personal information to any system.
